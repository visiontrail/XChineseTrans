# Verification of the added token-consumption claim

Checked 2026-10-18. This revises the existing draft; no publication was authorized or performed.

## Source chain and exact scope

- Matching news headline: Jiemian, https://www.jiemian.com/article/15193592.html . Some headlines use compute and R&D terminology that does not match the underlying categories.
- Underlying issuer document: COL Group / 中文在线集团股份有限公司, investor-relations activity record 2026-004. Meetings June 1–2, 2026; record dated June 3, 2026.
- Issuer-document PDF (hosted copy linked by the disclosure aggregator): https://infonotice.sylapp.cn/LC_NotTextAnnouncement/2026/06/03/833854873288.PDF
- Full HTML copy: https://www.9fzt.com/detail/sz_300364_9_89bdb29f210a09c8a659efe60930de16.html
- Section III, PDF pages 2–3: AI short dramas and video generation 55%; e-commerce and marketing 24%; software development 15%. Measure is token consumption. The document asserts domestic all-token coverage but does not disclose dataset, sample coverage, time window or cross-model/modality normalization methodology. Do not present these figures as independently verified national statistics.
- 55 + 24 = 79 percent. This may explain the user's recollection of over 70 percent, but that inference is not confirmation of the particular report they saw. E-commerce/marketing is not identical to entertainment short video.
- Software development is not equivalent to all scientific or industrial R&D. The 15 percent figure must not be described as all R&D.
- The disclosure does not establish a percentage of total national AI compute or a training-versus-inference allocation. Token use cannot by itself establish training-data quality or frontier capability.
- Company itself operates in digital entertainment; the figures are attributed to the company, not an independent census. Do not assert it measures only COL's own platform: the document does not say that.
- PDF pages 2–3 visually inspected; all 8 pages read as extracted text. No methodology or supporting statistical appendix was found; attachment list says none.

## Wang: avoid constructing a stronger claim than he made

- Publisher transcript: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/new-york-post_at-just-28-scale-ai-ceo-alexandr-wang-warns-activity-7316497394009821184-P4r4 . Wang's April 2025 comparison addresses compute, algorithms and data together. It is his historical assessment, not a current measured ranking.
- Wang's own September 24, 2024 interview: https://a16z.com/frontier-data-foundries-alex-wang-scale-ai/ . He explicitly stresses high-quality task-relevant data and measuring model weaknesses to identify which data to add. He also says much enterprise data is not relevant, while some is highly valuable.
- Editorial conclusion: data volume alone is insufficient evidence of overall AI leadership; the token-consumption report does not disprove Wang's three-pillar thesis. Entertainment can produce commercial value and feedback. Its share does not show resources are universally wasted or that research cannot progress.
