In this April 2025 interview, Alexandr Wang argued that the U.S. led on compute, the two countries were roughly level on algorithms, and China led on data.

I’d be careful about what “data advantage” means. In a June 2026 investor briefing, COL Group put AI short dramas and video generation at 55% of China’s token consumption, plus 24% for e-commerce and marketing. The disclosure offered no methodology; those figures don’t establish a share of national AI compute.

That doesn’t disprove Wang’s argument. Entertainment can generate revenue and useful feedback. But producing more content, consuming more tokens, and having better training data are different things. The advantage I’d look for is relevant, reliable data that improves a model’s ability to solve real problems. Volume alone doesn’t establish AI leadership.
