AI competition and the value of data

Alexandr Wang’s argument in this April 2025 interview: the U.S. led on compute, the two countries were roughly level on algorithms, and China led on data. For me, the useful question is what turns data into an advantage. Reliable labels, examples of real failures, and feedback from deployment matter more than a growing pile of files. That’s the system I’d want to build around a model.
