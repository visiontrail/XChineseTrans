This MIT-licensed project gives Claude Code and Codex an MCP toolkit to inspect JavaScript/Electron apps, decompile Android APK methods, and trace functions, strings and assembly in native binaries.

Its Notion case study follows a copy operation through Electron's preload and IPC into the system clipboard, then explains how copied blocks retain their structure.

I like being able to start with one concrete behavior and follow the evidence. That could be useful when you're maintaining unfamiliar software or learning how another app solves a problem.

Setup with a supported Node.js version and npm:
npx rea-agents@latest setup

Choose your agent, review the configuration changes, then restart it. Native analysis needs Ghidra, Hopper or IDA; Android analysis needs JADX and a JDK.
