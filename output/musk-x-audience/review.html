X and the value of the right audience

Complete source clip · 1:10 · English captions

X's strongest moat may be who pays attention here.

At Cannes Lions in 2024, Elon Musk argued that X is especially valuable for reaching people who run companies, allocate capital and shape decisions.

That is the part I find useful: if you're building a technical product, a small audience of the right founders, engineers and investors can matter more than a huge view count.

Write something worth their time. Give them a reason to start a conversation.
