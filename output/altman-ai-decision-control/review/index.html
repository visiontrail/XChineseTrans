Who keeps the ability to decide?

Altman's 2025 thought experiment still lands: follow the better recommendation, repeat, and gradually lose the ability to make the call yourself. Each decision could make sense on its own while leaving society dependent on a system it can't fully understand. Keeping humans in control means preserving the skills and authority to challenge the advice—and act without it.
