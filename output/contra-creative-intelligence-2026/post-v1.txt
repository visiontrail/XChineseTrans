AI makes it easier to produce creative work. Knowing what’s worth making—and getting it delivered well—still takes judgment.

Contra Labs’ 2026 report puts AI adoption at around 70% across experience levels and says project sizes for AI-powered creatives grew over 40%. That’s project size, not a 40% pay rise for everyone.

The interesting opportunity is in the services around the output: brand image systems, reusable prompt kits, workflow design and creative QA.

If you’re a designer, think beyond delivering one image. A consistent visual system your client’s team can use every week could be a much more useful thing to sell.
