Just discovered Impeccable, an open-source design skill that can give OpenAI models like those used in Codex more frontend design guidance and help move their interfaces beyond the usual cards-inside-cards look.

It captures product context, gives you focused commands like “bolder,” “distill,” and “audit,” and includes a local detector for common design issues. It also supports Claude Code, Cursor, and more.

https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
