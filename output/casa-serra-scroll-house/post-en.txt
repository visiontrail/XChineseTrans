A floor plan you can scroll into. Walls rise off the page, the camera walks through the front door, and line drawings give way to textured rooms.

The clever detail in Casa Serra: the plan is already a horizontal slice of the same 3D model. Raise the cut, move the camera, and the house reveals itself.

The workflow is worth borrowing: ask the coding agent for layout options and a camera route before it writes code. Pick one, build only as far as the first room, review the recording, then continue room by room.

The creator says the full demo took about 70 prompts over two weeks with Claude Code and Opus 5.5. A separate test of the shared starter prompt reached the first room in about an hour.

I can see this being useful for showing someone a house or hotel concept. Much easier to discuss a space when you can walk through it.
