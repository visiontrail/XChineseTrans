Meta unveiled Muse Charm at Connect: a pocket-sized device that puts its Muse AI agent on your keychain.

Tap the fingerprint sensor to talk, with a customizable avatar on screen. The pitch is simple: ask your AI something or show it your surroundings without unlocking your phone and opening an app.

Zuckerberg is targeting December shipping. It's still a prototype; pricing, battery life and connectivity details haven't been announced.

A faster way to reach an existing agent is a sensible idea. Whether it's worth carrying another device will depend on how useful it is day to day.
