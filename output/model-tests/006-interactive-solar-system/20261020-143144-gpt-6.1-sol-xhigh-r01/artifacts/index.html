A LITTLE PERSPECTIVE

Meet your neighborhood.

Orbit is a small, interactive atlas of the Sun and its eight planets. Choose a world, slow down, and look around.

A note on scale

Space is mostly space. To keep every planet within reach, the planets are enlarged and the distances compressed. Paths are circular, positions are illustrative, and moons are omitted. This is an explorable model, not an ephemeris.

The planets share one simulation clock. Their orbital periods are proportional to their real periods, so Mercury races around while Neptune takes its time. Planetary facts are rounded; one rotation is relative to the stars, and an AU is Earth's average distance from the Sun.
