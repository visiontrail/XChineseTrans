On RSI-Exam, the evaluated Grok 4.7 and Opus 5.5 agent setups score 0.3842 and 0.5357 respectively across 88 tasks. The benchmark tests agents improving executable methods or tools and checking them on unseen data; the underlying model weights stay frozen.

I’m interested in whether the new stack helps Grok run better experiments and turn them into reliable improvements. That would be a much more useful step than a bigger number on a spec sheet.
