Before Figure's F.02 robots jumped into molten steel, they practised landing on airbags.

Figure says the team used stunt performers' movements as a reference and trained a new AI motion model in simulation. Real robots then practised second-floor jumps at its San Jose campus, preparing for a foundry the team had never visited.

The company says the final jumps in Finland were autonomous. This clip puts the simulated motions beside the physical tests.

An unusual retirement project, with a real robotics problem at its centre: transferring a learned motion from simulation to hardware.
