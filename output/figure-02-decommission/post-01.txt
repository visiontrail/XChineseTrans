Figure gave its retired F.02 robots a Terminator 2 ending—with Arnold Schwarzenegger involved.

The company says it melted most of the fleet at a foundry in Imatra, Finland, to protect proprietary hardware as F.03 expands. A few units remain at HQ, and some of the recovered metal is being made into commemorative artifacts.

There is a practical reason behind the spectacle. Watching a humanoid lowered into molten steel, still giving a thumbs-up, feels unexpectedly sad.

If AI ever becomes conscious, this might be Exhibit A in its case against humanity.
