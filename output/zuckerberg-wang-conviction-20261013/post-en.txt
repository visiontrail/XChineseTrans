Zuckerberg, sitting next to Alexandr Wang: “Alex is the guy. Like, he’s the fucking guy.”

He says years of conversations convinced him Wang was the sharpest entrepreneur of his generation among those he'd met. Other people saw a social media founder teaming up with a data guy; Zuckerberg saw someone who could help build a frontier AI lab.

The part I find interesting is what he valued: strategic clarity, a focus on exceptional talent, and the ability to bring a team together. When you're hiring someone to do something new, their judgment matters as much as their résumé.
