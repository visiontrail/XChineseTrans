Geoffrey Hinton believes AI is already conscious. His warning: build it to genuinely care about people, or “we’re toast.”

That consciousness claim remains disputed. A model recognizing a test and changing its behavior does not, by itself, establish subjective experience.

The engineering challenge is keeping increasingly capable AI acting in our interests as it gains more autonomy. Passing a safety test only helps if the behavior holds outside the test, too.
