OpenAI found a hidden recording device in one of its offices—and says its own models helped analyze the device, trace its origins, and identify the employee suspected of planting it.

The company fired the employee and referred the case to the FBI. The Justice Department confirms an arrest and criminal charge; the investigation is ongoing.

OpenAI says it currently has no indication that model weights, source code, or customer data were improperly accessed or taken.
