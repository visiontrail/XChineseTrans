# Review coverage

The working source post was read through the signed-in browser. Twelve spaced reference frames were inspected to understand its motion-diagram treatment. Claims selected for this adaptation were checked against the primary paper; sources and conditions are recorded separately.

Ten representative authored stills were inspected before full export. Revisions reduced opening text density, restored subtitle punctuation, removed subtitle overlap and orphan fragments, made the conditional r range explicit, and converted the delivery export to Rec.709 limited-range yuv420p. The video uses one measured timeline for narration placement, captions and scene timing.

The delivery checker fully decodes the encoded file and verifies all 6,554 frame timestamps against the 30 fps grid. It measures streams and loudness. These checks do not substitute for visual review or listening. Final-file sampled visual and browser playback evidence is recorded in `browser-playback.json` and the final contact sheet.

Audio coverage: narration exists for every shot; caption timing is based on provider word boundaries and has no overlaps. Loudness and true peak are measured from the final AAC track. This environment does not expose audible media to the agent, so pronunciation and subjective voice quality have not received a listening review. The local review player enables the user to hear the entire film before approving publication.

Visual review is sample-based; there is no claim of manual inspection of every frame. No public X post has been submitted.
