Sam Altman was already betting on a one-person company valued at $1B back in 2024. That’s an ambitious prediction. The useful takeaway for me is how much a small team could get done.

Give AI a defined role, persistent project context and a clear finish line. Start with a recurring job—research, code changes or tests—that you can inspect and roll back. Keep judgment and final decisions with you.

Then build a workflow that can repeat reliably: tasks, tools, memory and checks that survive beyond one chat.

A subscription gives you access. What you build around it determines how much useful work gets done.
