An internet connection can be a classroom, a shopfront, and a way to reach customers far beyond your hometown.

Elon Musk argues that getting people online is one of the biggest ways to help them out of poverty: they can learn for free and sell their goods or services to the global market. Starlink aims to bring that access to places where broadband is missing or expensive.

I like the focus on learning and earning. The real value is what you can do with the connection: learn a skill, build a service, or sell what you make.
