America.gov gives U.S. government information a single AI entry point, drawing on more than 29,000 government websites.

Ask in plain language how to get a passport for your child, understand Medicare eligibility, apply for veterans’ benefits, or find a national park campsite. The service provides guidance with links to official sources.

Free to use. No account required. No ads.

The current release focuses on answers and guidance. It cannot access agency records or check your application status. Form completion and progress tracking are planned for 2027.

What interests me is the interface design: people can start with what they need to do, without first figuring out which agency or website handles it.

The real test will be whether it gives accurate guidance and reduces the steps needed to complete a task.
