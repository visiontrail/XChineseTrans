Kenneth thinks “Hey Neuralink,” followed by a task. A Grok agent then gets to work on his laptop.

In this Neuralink prototype, a brain implant records activity associated with intended speech. A decoder turns it into text that the agent can act on. In the demo, Kenneth asks it to find a YouTube video.

A short request could replace a whole sequence of clicks. For someone with speech or movement impairments, that could make everyday computer use much more accessible. Still an early prototype, but a compelling direction for AI agents.
