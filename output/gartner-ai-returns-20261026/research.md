## Primary evidence

https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-10-20-gartner-says-44-percent-of-ceos-struggle-to-demonstrate-ai-financial-returns-as-technology-becomes-central-to-growth

Official release date: October 20, 2026. Survey fieldwork: April–May 2026. Sample: 203 global CEOs and senior business executives. The release calls it the 2027 Gartner CEO Survey, although its linked client-report title says 2026. The draft avoids that inconsistent survey-year label.

Verified findings used in the draft:

- 44% of CEOs struggle to demonstrate AI-driven financial returns to stakeholders. This does not establish zero returns or an inability to calculate all returns.
- 75% plan to increase technology investment. This is a stated plan, not observed spending, and it is not limited to AI.
- 77% expect AI industrialization to have medium or high impact on strategic business objectives through 2027.
- 20% report high readiness for AI industrialization. This is a different response threshold from the medium-or-high impact measure; the percentages are not complements and do not measure the same construct.
- Gartner advocates redesigning workflows and decisions around AI capabilities, with financial and operational outcome measures.

Other original-post table entries were checked: economic volatility 93% medium/high impact and 24% high readiness; persistent conflict 79% and 11%. Omitted from the draft to keep the focus on AI.
