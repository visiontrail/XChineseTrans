44% of CEOs struggle to show stakeholders a financial return from AI. Yet 75% plan to increase technology investment, according to Gartner's survey of 203 CEOs and senior executives.

The readiness gap is striking too: 77% expect AI industrialization to have a medium or high impact on their business objectives through 2027, while only 20% report high readiness.

This is where forward-deployed engineers can earn their keep. Plugging a model into a workflow may speed up one step while leaving the real bottleneck untouched.

The useful work is redesigning the process with the people who run it: decide what AI handles, who owns exceptions, and how to measure the result. Start with one workflow and track cost per completed case, turnaround time, and rework.

Buying AI tools is a budget decision. Turning them into better business outcomes is an operating decision.
