Gartner survey of 203 global CEOs and senior executives: 44% struggle to demonstrate AI's financial returns to stakeholders; 75% plan to increase technology investment. For AI industrialization through 2027, 77% expect medium or high impact on business objectives and 20% report high readiness. Impact and readiness are different survey measures. Takeaway: redesign the workflow and measure the business result. Survey conducted April–May 2026; findings released October 20, 2026.
