Claude initially mistook a car-insurance claim for a skiing accident. The fix was to give it the context it was missing.

Anthropic’s Prompting 101 walks through a Swedish accident form and a handwritten sketch. The useful lessons:

• Explain the form and messy human markings.
• Read the form first, then cross-check the sketch.
• Ask it to flag unclear evidence instead of guessing.
• Define the output your application actually needs.

It’s a 2025 session, so check model-specific advice: newer Claude models no longer support assistant-response prefill.

If you’re building document workflows, this is a useful reminder: turn the judgment you take for granted into clear instructions, then test them on difficult cases.
