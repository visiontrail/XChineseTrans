Gemini subscriptions are easier to compare when you separate model access from usage limits.

Google's public help page currently lists Flash-Lite, Flash and Pro for all four tiers.

Usage allowances:
• Free: standard
• AI Plus: 2× standard
• AI Pro: 4× standard
• AI Ultra: 5× or 20× AI Pro, depending on the subscription

These are compute-based limits: the task, model, thinking level and conversation length affect consumption. They refresh every five hours, subject to a weekly cap.

Check your account's model picker and usage page before choosing a plan. This reflects public documentation as of October 4, 2026; availability and limits can change.
