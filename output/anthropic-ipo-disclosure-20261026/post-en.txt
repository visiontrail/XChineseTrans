Anthropic could make its IPO filing public as early as October 26, according to The Information. A mid-November listing is reportedly planned; the timing remains unconfirmed.

The financial details deserve a closer look. Reuters reports $16.8B in Q3 revenue, up 45% from Q2, based on an investor presentation. WSJ’s draft-filing report puts the first-half net loss at $80.7B, with roughly $72B tied to revaluing financing instruments. That headline loss is not the same as cash burned.

The cloud relationships matter just as much. Reuters found that Amazon and Google’s marketplaces handled 47% of Anthropic’s 2025 revenue, collecting distribution fees averaging about 16 cents per dollar of those sales. Those partners also supply compute, invest in Anthropic and compete with it.

A public S-1 should help show how much of that growth can become durable cash flow after compute costs and distribution fees. Any claim of “adjusted profitability” needs a clear explanation of which costs are excluded.
