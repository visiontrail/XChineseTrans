## Verified claims and editorial corrections

1. Anthropic H1 2026 revenue: $16.286B, rounded $16.3B; net loss: $80.748B, rounded $80.7B. The S-1 describes $72.388B (rounded $72.4B) of changes in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs, primarily resulting from valuation increases. This is broader than convertible notes alone. Do not call the net loss cash burn or subtract one accounting line to infer cash spent.
2. Operating cash flow: +$3.929B in H1 2026. Management attributes improvement partly to working-capital timing, including receipts and payments. This does not establish free cash flow, sustained profitability or absence of future funding requirements. Investing cash outflow includes marketable securities and cannot simply be labeled spending on compute.
3. SpaceXAI contracts: up to $120.5B over terms extending into 2029, about $3.1B per month once all capacity is delivered (expected December 2026 in the filing). Initial non-cancelable periods end January and March 2027 under the respective agreements, after which either party can cancel on 90 days' advance written notice. Do not present $120.5B as an unconditional non-cancelable payment obligation.
4. SpaceX: Customer B generated 34.5% of Q3 2026 consolidated revenue and is associated with the SI segment. The table does not identify this customer as Anthropic. This is not 34.5% of the compute business. The draft preserves the unnamed-customer fact and does not assert the identity or a cash collection figure.
5. The S-1 warns growth rates may decline and additional financing may be needed sooner than anticipated. These are risk disclosures, not proof of an imminent funding shortfall. The closing paragraph is our analysis of the customer/supplier relationship.
6. Omitted: a confirmed November trading date, $2T valuation / $100B raise as settled terms, the unsupported characterization of nearly $9B as actual H1 compute/infrastructure cash spending, and Musk's broad claim about all neocloud borrowers.
