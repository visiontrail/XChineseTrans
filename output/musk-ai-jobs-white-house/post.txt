Elon Musk’s answer to AI job fears: “universal high income.”

Speaking outside the White House on September 29, he said AI and robotics could lead to an “age of abundance,” with better healthcare and higher living standards for everyone.

Asked what happens if people have no jobs, he said roles will evolve. “Computer” once meant a person doing calculations; digital computers took over that work. He also pointed to strong current demand for human labor.

His forecast is optimistic. This exchange offered no timeline or concrete plan for making universal high income happen.

The question I’d focus on is the transition: how do people whose work is automated share in the productivity gains, and what supports them along the way?
