How Cursor Turned AI Agents Into Better Engineers

Part of Become an AI Native Builder

60 min
Aug 12, 2026 12:00 PM
Virtual (Zoom)

In this video

What you'll learn

Why AI agents behave like talented but forgetful new hires

How to set up your agent for success with engineering use cases

The skills and workflows Cursor engineers rely on daily

How skills like /architect and /tdd improve engineering

Live examples of turning messy tasks into reliable workflows

Practical techniques for building your own engineering team's AI operating system

Why this topic matters

AI agents are powerful, but they're forgetful, inconsistent, and prone to making the same mistakes over and over. In this workshop, Lauren will share the engineering skills framework used internally at Cursor and now open sourced as pstack. You'll learn how to equip AI with reusable playbooks, decision-making frameworks, review processes, and debugging workflows that improve output quality.
