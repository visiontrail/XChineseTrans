Google’s CC is becoming a shared AI agent for household admin.

Up to six people can work with one agent, which has its own verified Google account. Each person chooses which emails, files and calendars to share.

The useful part is connecting information to action. In Google’s demos, CC turns a swimming lesson email into a calendar event with the location, equipment and waiver reminder. It also pre-fills a camp registration PDF, asks the family for a missing emergency contact, then returns the updated form for review before sending.

That targets a familiar bottleneck: the repeated forwarding, follow-ups and confirmations needed to get a household task finished.

The permission boundary matters. Google says CC will not act or share information outside the group without permission.

Still an early Google Labs experiment: U.S., 18+, personal Google accounts, with a waitlist for new users.
