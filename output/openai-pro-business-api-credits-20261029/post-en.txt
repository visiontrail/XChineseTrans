Your ChatGPT subscription can now help fund the apps you build.

Eligible Pro subscribers can claim $100, $200, or $500 in monthly API credits, depending on their plan. Business credits are pooled across paid seats, up to $500 per workspace per month. Plus isn’t eligible.

Claim the benefit in Settings → Billing and link an API organization you own. Your subscription must be at least seven days old, and the first grant is prorated.

Credits expire at the end of the billing cycle. They don’t increase ChatGPT or signed-in Codex limits, and can’t be used for Codex via API key.
