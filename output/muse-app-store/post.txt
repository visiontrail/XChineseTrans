Meta's Muse is now #1 on the U.S. iPhone App Store's free-app chart.

What interests me most is distribution. Meta already owns WhatsApp, and users can talk to Muse directly there. That gives its personal AI agent a familiar entry point into everyday life.

If Meta can turn those conversations into useful actions, the opportunity is huge. The next test is retention: can Muse save enough time to become a daily habit?
