Cheaper inference doesn’t automatically mean faster answers.

A circulating Azure screenshot describes GPT-6.1 Sol as using two inference passes instead of three. That wording isn’t in the current model card, so the architecture claim still needs confirmation.

Even if accurate, it wouldn’t establish that Sol should be faster than Astra. End-to-end latency also depends on token generation, serving conditions and tool calls.

I’d compare quality, time to complete the task and total cost. A lower token price loses its appeal if retries eat up the savings.
