Sonnet 5.5’s low token price can hide an expensive Max-effort run.

API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Anthropic reports 30%+ faster output generation and up to 30% lower task costs than Sonnet 5 in its testing.

The trade-off changes with effort. In Artificial Analysis’ published evaluation, Sonnet 5.5 (Max)† scored 64% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, versus 60% for Opus 5.5 (Max)†. That lead is specific to this benchmark and evaluation setup.

Across the AA Intelligence Index, Sonnet 5.5 (Max)† used about 193k output tokens per task—roughly 60% more than Opus 5.5 (Max)†. AA’s weighted average task cost was $7.60 versus $5.98. The attached chart shows token use; the dollar figures come from AA’s separate cost metric.

My practical take: start at Medium for well-defined work. Increase effort when a harder task needs it, and measure correctness, elapsed time, and total cost. Make Max earn its place in the workflow.

These launch results used a pre-release Sonnet deployment with a since-fixed structured-output bug; AA says it will rerun relevant evaluations.

† Fallback enabled: some tasks may be handled by other Claude models.
