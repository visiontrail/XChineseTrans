An Unreal Engine boss fight built with an AI-assisted workflow: Opus 5.5 for code, GPT Astra for images and textures, Tripo for 3D models, and a mix of tools for animation.

The interesting part is the pipeline behind the demo. The creator also released video2rig, a tool that turns generated motion video into editable animation on a 3D rig in Blender.
