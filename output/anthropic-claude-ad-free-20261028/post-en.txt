Anthropic’s founders have reaffirmed their commitment to keep Claude ad-free in a letter accompanying its IPO prospectus.

It’s a smart distinction: Anthropic makes its money from usage and subscriptions, while OpenAI is testing ads in ChatGPT. For users, the trade-off is worth watching: keeping ads out doesn’t make the cost of AI disappear.
