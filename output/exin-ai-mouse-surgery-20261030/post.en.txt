An AI agent reportedly chose a gene therapy from 19 candidates, selected a brain target, and controlled the injection in a mouse model of epilepsy. That's a striking step from suggesting experiments to carrying them out.

A human performed the craniotomy, and a vet approved every irreversible step. The mouse reportedly recovered; seizure reduction hasn't been demonstrated here. If this becomes reliable, it could shorten the loop from a treatment hypothesis to an animal experiment.
