AI coding can make source control the bottleneck.

Anthropic says daily merges grew from roughly 2,500 in May to over 30,000 in September. Its response was Scion: move more work to servers, let always-online agents request history and diffs through APIs, and fetch files only when needed.

The interesting question for other teams is where their own pipeline starts queuing up: checkout, review, tests, or deployment. Faster code generation only helps if the rest can absorb it.

Scion targets an unusually large monorepo. Anthropic explicitly says it isn’t a general-purpose Git replacement.
