Before plugging an AI reply bot into X, read the automation rules.

X requires prior written approval for AI-powered reply bots. Automated replies also need the recipient to opt in, an easy way to opt out, and a limit of one reply per interaction. Following your account alone doesn't count as consent.

But how can X—or any of us—tell? As models improve and AI sounds more human, do restrictions like these still make sense?
