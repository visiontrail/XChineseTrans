Infographic titled “Great demo. Reliable agent?” Four questions: What counts as success? The actual outcome meets predefined requirements; verify the outcome rather than the completion message. Can it repeat that success? Repeat the task in clean environments. Can you trust the grader? Weak checks can accept bad work and rigid checks can reject valid work; inspect actual runs. Does it match user needs? Turn real failures into regression tests and rerun when models, prompts or tools change. Takeaway: make limits visible and catch regressions early.
