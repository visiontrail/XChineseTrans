Finding a vulnerability is the start of the work.

Anthropic’s disclosure dashboard, updated October 2, reports 6,157 findings sent to maintainers across 591 open-source projects, with 516 known to have been patched.

Those figures cover multiple Claude models. They also mix independently triaged reports with direct disclosures, so they are not a count of independently confirmed Mythos-only bugs.

One detail worth reading carefully: “acknowledged” means a maintainer responded. It does not mean a fix shipped. And a patch upstream does not mean users have installed it.

AI can accelerate discovery while validation, maintainer capacity, and deployment still determine how much risk actually gets removed.

The useful dashboard is the whole pipeline, not just the biggest number at the top.
