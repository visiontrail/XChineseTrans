DigUp lets you find a file by describing what’s inside it: a moment in a video, a passage in a PDF, or something said in a recording.

It uses Google’s EmbeddingGemma 2 to search text, images, audio and video locally, alongside exact keyword matches. You choose which folders it indexes.

Free and open source. Mac requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later; Windows and Linux are in beta. The project says indexed files stay on your computer, though the app connects to download the model and check for updates.

https://github.com/ARahim3/DigUp
