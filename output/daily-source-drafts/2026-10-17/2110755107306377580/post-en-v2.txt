GitHub's AI Scan became generally available on October 15. It checks pull requests for security issues in areas CodeQL doesn't cover, including PHP, Shell scripts, Terraform and Dockerfiles.

Its findings are advisory and cannot block merges through rulesets.

That leaves me wondering: if the AI checking the code is less capable than the AI writing it, could subtle vulnerabilities slip through?
