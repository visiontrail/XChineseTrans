If you’re prototyping a scanned-form review tool, Jev 1.14.0’s new Box type could locate a signature so your app can crop it for a reviewer.

The catch: Box returns coordinates even when the target is missing. Pair it with a separate presence check. A valid box isn’t proof the right object was found.

TypeSafe also unlocked images in state, instructions, and criteria, plus optional 262K-token context. Those work on 1.13 too; Box needs 1.14. The company expects the biggest model improvements in multilingual support.

Images, Box, and long context are experimental features behind “danger zone” headers. TypeSafe isn’t vouching for their production use. 1.14 is currently on jev-preview.
