Illustrated Jev 1.14 workflow for reviewing signatures on scanned forms. First ask a separate Noul question whether a signature is present. If absent or uncertain, skip the crop or send the form for review. If present, ask one Box question to locate the signature, then crop it for human review. A note warns that Box returns a bounding box even when the target is missing, so coordinates alone do not prove detection. Images and Box are experimental.
