Restaurant data is a useful reality check for browser agents. Getting the menu quickly matters less if the prices need fixing afterward.

The task was to collect restaurant menus, prices, and reviews from Google Maps.

In Josh’s informal test, DeepSeek in its own harness tied Opus at 29.5 points, but took 9–16 minutes per place. Opus in Chrome took 4–7; Sol in fast mode took about 3, scoring 27 points. An interesting workflow comparison, though the different browsers and modes make it hard to isolate model quality.
