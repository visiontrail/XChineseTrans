Google Cloud customers will soon be able to use existing cloud spending commitments to buy OpenAI models.

GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna are rolling out in private preview for selected enterprises, alongside support for Codex workflows. General availability is planned in the coming weeks, with Antigravity integration also on the way.

For teams already on Google Cloud, that’s a useful shift: more model choice within the cloud budget and tools they already use. Competing on models, partnering on distribution.

Google AI Pro/Ultra access hasn’t been announced.
