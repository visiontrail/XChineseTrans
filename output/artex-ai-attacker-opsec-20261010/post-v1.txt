An attacker used AI to target South Korean financial institutions—and left their own AI work logs on the public internet.

CrowdStrike says open directories exposed Claude Code session histories, ARTEX configs and memory files, giving researchers a detailed trail of the operation.

The strangest detail: one session asked for a security researcher résumé that included the results of the same activity. That conversation also contained personal details, though CrowdStrike says they cannot definitively link them to the attacker.

AI can make someone faster at attacking systems. It doesn't give them better judgment about securing their own.
