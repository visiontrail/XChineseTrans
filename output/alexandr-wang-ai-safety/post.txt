AI safety has a very human coordination problem.

Alexandr Wang tells Cleo Abram that personal feuds and mistrust among AI leaders are shaping how the technology gets built. He points to internal controls, external audits, and board-level oversight as practical steps toward rebuilding trust.

That’s the part I find useful. Safety needs processes that hold up even when lab leaders don’t like—or trust—one another. Brilliant people aren’t a substitute for a system others can verify.
