Brake safety is the baseline for any carmaker.

Dongchedi reported broken brake-pedal brackets on all three Maextro V800s it tested during repeated emergency stops from 100 km/h. Maextro calls the conditions non-standard and extreme, says no such failures have occurred in customer use, and has promised design improvements and free upgrades.

Mistakes can happen as an industry develops. But safety is a line we cannot move to accommodate them. Owners deserve a transparent investigation, independent retesting and a clear fix. The real measure of progress is whether people can trust the car when they need it most.
