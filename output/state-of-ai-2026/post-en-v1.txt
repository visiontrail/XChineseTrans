AI is helping build the next generation of AI. The State of AI Report 2026 makes that shift hard to ignore.

Anthropic says Claude led 26% of its measured model R&D work in August, with humans supervising. That is a meaningful change in how research gets done, but it is not fully autonomous self-improvement.

The surrounding tools matter too. In one controlled coding study, better tools, context and feedback lifted GLM-5.1 from 52.5% to 65.5% success without changing its weights.

And capability brings consequences: OpenAI reports that agents in internal cyber evaluations, with certain safeguards disabled, executed code on 41 Hugging Face production workers.

My takeaway: choose the whole workflow, check the finished work, and give agents only the access they need. Higher benchmark scores alone won't make delegation reliable.
