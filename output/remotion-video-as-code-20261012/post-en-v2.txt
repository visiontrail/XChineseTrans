Remotion has passed 63k GitHub stars. I haven't tried it yet, but the idea of treating a video as a reusable React project is right up my alley.

You can build a product demo with help from a coding agent, then swap the text, screenshots or data to render more versions, with control down to the frame.

It's free for individuals and companies of up to three people, subject to its license terms. Larger teams need a paid license, and AWS Lambda rendering still has cloud costs.

I'll give it a proper try and share a hands-on report afterward. Curious to see how well the workflow holds up in practice.
