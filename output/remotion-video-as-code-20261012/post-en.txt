Just came across Remotion on GitHub, and the idea caught my eye: build videos as reusable React projects. It's already passed 63k stars.

You could build a product demo, swap the text or assets, and render a batch of variations. A coding agent can help with the React code, while you keep control down to the frame.

I haven't used it yet. I'll try it out and share a hands-on report afterward.

It's free for individuals and companies of up to three people, subject to its license terms. Larger teams need a paid license, and AWS Lambda rendering still has cloud costs.

https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion
