In this May 2026 Forbes interview, Musk predicts that AI will exceed humanity’s combined intelligence by 2031, alongside at least 100 million humanoid robots. He also expects the economy to roughly double within five to seven years.

An aggressive forecast. My takeaway is practical: use AI to build something you own—a useful product, an audience that trusts you, and a way to reach customers.

Start with one problem people will pay to solve. Use AI to handle the repeatable work, so growth doesn’t always require another hour of your time.

AI can make building cheaper. You still need to make something people want.
