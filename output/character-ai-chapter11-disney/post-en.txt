Character.AI has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. A sobering turn for one of the early breakout names in consumer AI.

Disney’s proposed $20 million deal includes a non-exclusive, perpetual license to some of its IP and remains subject to higher or better offers. It doesn’t guarantee the app’s survival.

Character.AI says the platform will keep running during the sale process. If no buyer provides for its continued operation, it will be wound down.

For AI companions, safety and legal risk belong in the business model from day one. You can’t treat them as cleanup work after growth.
