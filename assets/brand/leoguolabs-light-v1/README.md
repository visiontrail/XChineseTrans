## User direction, September 28, 2026

Use FrontierTechSN's light palette as the starting point: warm ivory, green text and gold emphasis. The user subsequently rejected leaves, gradients and extra ornament, and requested simple modern technical sans-serif typography consistent with prior X images. The latest background correction replaces the harsh solid-yellow field with the subtle natural warm-ivory paper feel of the user's first attached reference. Delicate, low-contrast paper fiber or grain is now intentional and supersedes the earlier texture ban. These explicit corrections take precedence over the source project's serif headings, gradients, cards and shadows.

Use a soft warm-ivory canvas around `#FDFBF7`, delicate and evenly distributed paper fiber or fine grain, Geist/Inter-like typography, restrained dark-gold emphasis, clear alignment, whitespace and sparse straight rules. Pale yellow supplies only slight warmth or a small optional accent. No serif display faces, designed gradients, glow, shadows, foliage, badges, generic icons or decorative panels. Avoid distressed paper, stains, creases, conspicuous digital noise and broad saturated or uniformly yellow backgrounds. The texture must stay subtle enough to preserve crisp text.
